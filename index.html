Where are my notes stored?

On your iPhone, as ordinary Markdown (.md) files. Open the Files app → On My iPhone → Folio to see them. You can also open any folder on your device, such as an Obsidian vault, from the workspace menu.

How do I sync with my Mac?

Install Folio on your Mac, open Settings → iPhone Sync to get a 6-digit code, then on your iPhone open Settings → Mac Sync → Pair with Mac… and enter the code. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Notes sync directly between your devices — no account or server is involved. Sync is optional; the app works fully without it.

What happens if I edit the same note on both devices?

Edits to different lines are merged automatically. If the same line was changed on both, the Mac version keeps the original name and the iPhone version is saved alongside it as a "conflict" copy, so nothing is lost.

How do I move a card on a board?

Long-press the card and choose Move To.

I deleted a note by mistake

Deleted notes go to the Folio Trash folder first, so you can restore them from the Files app.
