Privacy Policy

Folio Vault · Effective November 14, 2026

Short version: Folio Vault does not collect, store or share any of your data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.

Your notes

Your notes, boards, reminders and attachments are stored only on your device, in folders you control. The developer never receives them.

Mac sync

If you choose to pair Folio Vault with Folio on your Mac, notes are sent directly between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi network using an encrypted connection. They do not pass through any server. The app asks for Local Network permission only for this purpose.

Link previews

When you add a web link to a note, the app may fetch that page to show its title and image. That request goes directly from your device to the website you linked, which will see an ordinary web request. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your iPhone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
